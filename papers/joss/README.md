# JOSS manuscript preparation (NOT SUBMITTED)

This folder contains **draft material only**, prepared against the JOSS paper guidelines. It does not mean that CIFC is eligible for review.

- **paper.md**: unfinished manuscript with JOSS-format YAML and required sections.
- **paper.bib**: verified bibliographic metadata for selected foundational sources.

## Hard blockers (11 November 2026)

1. **Public repository history:** JOSS currently requires **more than six months** of public, distributed project development. CIFC was created on 11 November 2026; rapid commits or a repository dump do not satisfy this.
2. **Feature-complete research software:** the current demonstration is a compact script rather than an installable, maintainable general-purpose research package.
3. **Research impact:** no verified independent adoption or substantial demonstrated research application exists yet.
4. **Full comparisons:** the periodic baseline performs at least as well as CIFC on the documented toy example; stronger nonperiodic and real-hardware benchmarks are needed.
5. **Independent scientific review:** mathematical assumptions, proof, numerical bounds, literature coverage, and software behavior must be checked by qualified people.
6. **Author information:** verify public affiliation, contributor list, financial support, and all AI tools and versions; the existing draft **does not assert** completed human validation.

## Submission process (only after the blockers are addressed)

Review current guidelines at https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html and https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/paper.html . JOSS papers emphasize the **software** rather than claiming a new scientific theorem. Submit through https://joss.theoj.org only when eligible. JOSS will review the paper **and software** via public GitHub issues; authors must correspond with reviewers themselves (JOSS restricts conversational use of AI in those interactions).

Do not suggest that the existing Zenodo DOI signifies JOSS acceptance; it archives software, not an accepted journal article.
